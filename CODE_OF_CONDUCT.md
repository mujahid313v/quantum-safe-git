# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in QSGit is expected to treat others with respect. Keep discussion focused on the work, make room for different experience levels, and criticize ideas or code without attacking people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, sexualized attention, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Do not retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

The maintainer may ask someone to stop, remove harmful content, or restrict participation when needed. The response will take the impact and repeated behavior into account.

For a conduct report, contact the maintainer through GitHub at `mujahid313v`. Share enough detail to investigate and avoid posting private information publicly. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.
